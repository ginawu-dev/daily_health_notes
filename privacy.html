Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Health Notes handles information when you use the app.

Information stored in the app

Daily Health Notes allows you to record health information such as symptoms, body temperature, blood pressure, medical tests, medications, reminders, notes, and family-member profiles.

This information is stored locally on your device. Daily Health Notes does not require an account and does not send your health records to our servers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app uses Apple’s local notification system to notify you about medications and medical-test appointments. These notifications are scheduled on your device.

Exporting and sharing data

You may choose to export or share your records using the iOS sharing features. Exporting or sharing is initiated by you. Please take care when choosing where exported files are stored or sent.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect or sell your health information. The app does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or user accounts.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual health entries, medications, and family profiles within the app. You can also remove the app from your device to delete locally stored app data, subject to iOS backup behavior.

Children’s privacy

Daily Health Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account or transmit health records to our servers, no personal information is collected through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: ginawuwriter@outlook.com

Important health notice

Daily Health Notes is intended for personal health tracking only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice.
