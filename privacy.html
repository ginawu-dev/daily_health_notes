Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Daily Health Notes is a personal health journal for iPhone and iPad. This Privacy Policy explains how the app and public website handle information in the context of the available public information for Daily Health Notes.

The current App Store privacy disclosure for Daily Health Notes states: “Data Not Collected.” This repository contains only website files and no app implementation, so this policy is written conservatively using verified public information only. No developer-operated health server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or other app-side data collection infrastructure is evident in the available repository files.

Health Information

Daily Health Notes allows users to record personal health information such as symptoms, symptom significance levels, body temperature, blood pressure, medical-test records, personal health notes, and everyday health journal entries. This information is recorded by the user for personal tracking purposes.

Medication Information

The app supports medication records, daily or weekly medication schedules, and medication reminders. Users can also record medications without reminders. This information is part of the user’s personal health journal and stays within the app’s local device-based workflow unless the user chooses an export or sync option.

Menstrual Cycle Information

The app includes menstrual cycle tracking features such as period start and end dates, flow levels, intimate-day tracking, calendar period ranges, and upcoming-period predictions. This data is recorded as part of the user’s personal health notes and cycle history when used.

Family Profiles

The app supports separate family profiles. Each profile can have its own entries, medications, history, trends, and applicable cycle data. The data is organized by profile for personal record keeping.

Local Storage

Daily Health Notes stores health and related information locally on the user’s device. This includes the personal records users create in the app, such as health notes, symptoms, medications, and cycle details. Local storage is managed by the app on the device and is not confirmed to be transmitted to the developer’s servers based on the available public information.

iCloud Sync

The app can sync records through the user’s private iCloud database when iCloud is available. There is also a Sync Now option for retrieving records when setting up a new device. This uses Apple’s iCloud infrastructure rather than an app-specific server described in the repository or App Store disclosure.

Backup Export

Users can export a backup of their records. Exporting is a user-initiated action. If a user shares or stores that backup elsewhere, the handling of that backup is outside the app’s direct control and should be managed with care.

Notifications

If medication reminders or medical-test appointment reminders are enabled, the app uses Apple’s local notification system on the device to schedule reminders. These notifications are device-based and are not described as a developer-side tracking system in the available public information.

Third-Party Services

The available repository contains only website files, and no app implementation source code was available for review. Based on the public App Store privacy disclosure and the website files, there is no evidence in the available material of advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, developer-operated servers, or other third-party data collection services being used by the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users can delete entries, medications, family profiles, and other app records within the app. Records can also be removed from the device when the app is deleted, subject to standard iOS backup and device behavior. If a user exports a backup, the backup remains under the user’s control and should be managed according to their own storage and sharing choices.

Children’s Privacy

Daily Health Notes is a personal health journal intended for personal record keeping. The public information available does not show any developer-side collection of children’s personal data. The app is not described as requiring an account or a developer-controlled data store.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app or public disclosures change. The latest version will be made available on this page.
