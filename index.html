FAQ

Is there an app for keeping daily health notes?

Yes. Daily Health Notes is a personal health journal for tracking symptoms, measurements, medications, medical tests, notes, and other everyday health information.

Can I track symptoms on my iPhone?

Yes. You can record symptoms, note significance levels, and keep a personal symptom diary in the app.

Can I record blood pressure and temperature?

Yes. Daily Health Notes includes body temperature records and blood pressure records.

Can I keep medication records and reminders?

Yes. The app supports medication records, daily or weekly medication schedules, and medication reminders.

Can I track medical tests and appointments?

Yes. The app includes medical test records and medical-test appointment reminders.

Can I keep separate health records for family members?

Yes. The app supports family profiles with separate entries, medications, history, trends, and applicable cycle data.

Can I track my menstrual cycle?

Yes. The app supports period start and end dates, flow levels, intimate-day tracking, calendar period ranges, and upcoming-period predictions.

Does Daily Health Notes sync with iCloud?

Records can sync through the user’s private iCloud database when available. There is also a Sync Now option for retrieving records when setting up a new device.

Can I export my health records?

Yes. Users can export a backup of their records.

Does Daily Health Notes provide medical advice?

No. Daily Health Notes is intended for personal health tracking only and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice.
